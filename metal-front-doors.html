What They Are

What Is a Custom Iron Front Door?

A custom iron front door is an entry door with a welded steel or wrought iron slab and frame, built to the measurements of a specific opening rather than to a stock size. Most include tempered glass behind decorative ironwork, insulation and a thermal break inside the slab, and weather sealing around the perimeter. They are chosen for three reasons: security, longevity, and the fact that an entry is the first thing anyone sees.

Because they are made to fit, they are the practical answer for arched openings, oversized entries, and older houses where nothing off the shelf lines up.

Signature Look

Arched & Round-Top Iron Doors

An arched entry is where a custom door earns its keep. Stock doors do not come curved, so an arched opening either gets squared off with a filler panel — which never looks right — or gets a door built to the actual radius.

We template the opening on site and form the arch to match. Full round-top, eyebrow, and segmental arches are all buildable.

  • Full round-top, eyebrow, and segmental arches
  • Hand-formed scrollwork over tempered glass
  • Operable glass panels for ventilation
  • Suits Spanish, Mediterranean and Tuscan architecture

Wide Entries

Double Iron Entry Doors

A pair of doors suits a wide opening better than one oversized slab, and it gives you the option of opening one leaf day to day and both when you are moving furniture or hosting.

Divided-light glass with solid lower panels is a popular configuration — daylight into the entry, privacy at floor level, and a look that reads modern or traditional depending on the grid.

  • Active and inactive leaf with flush bolts
  • Divided-light or single-pane glazing
  • Solid lower panels for privacy
  • Matching sidelights and transoms available

Before You Buy

Three Things Worth Knowing First

Insulation Is Not Optional

The reputation iron doors have for being hot comes from thin uninsulated slabs. A properly built door has a thermal break and insulation inside it. Ask about this before you compare prices, because it is often where a cheap quote is cheap.

The Finish Depends on Your Exposure

A west-facing entry two blocks from the beach in Oxnard needs a different finish spec than a shaded entry inland in Ojai. Salt and UV are the two enemies. We spec for your actual site, not a default.

Measure Before You Fall in Love

Photos online are usually of a different opening size. Before getting attached to a design, have the opening measured — some looks simply do not scale to a narrow entry, and it is better to know early.

Common Questions

Iron Front Door FAQs

How much does a custom iron front door cost?

Cost depends on the size of the opening, single versus double, whether the top is arched or square, how much ornamental ironwork is involved, and the glass and hardware selected. A clean square-top single door costs considerably less than an arched double with hand-formed scrollwork. We measure the opening and provide a free written estimate before any work begins, and financing is available.

Do iron front doors make a house hotter?

A properly built iron door should not. Modern iron entry doors are built with a thermal break and insulation inside the door slab, and weather sealing around the perimeter, so they perform comparably to other quality entry doors. The doors that develop a reputation for being hot are typically thin uninsulated slabs without a thermal break.

Are iron front doors secure?

Yes, and security is one of the main reasons people choose them. A welded steel or iron door slab in a steel frame is substantially harder to force than a hollow-core or timber door. Any glass is tempered and typically protected behind ironwork, and we fit multi-point locking hardware where you want the extra security.

Can you build an iron door to fit my existing opening?

Yes. Everything is fabricated to the measurements of your actual opening rather than to a stock size, which matters most on arched and older openings where nothing off the shelf fits. If you would rather change the opening — going from a single to a double, or adding an arch — that is possible too, though it becomes a construction job as well as a door job.

How do iron doors hold up near the coast?

Well, provided the finish is right for the exposure. Salt air is hard on metal, so coastal properties in Ventura, Oxnard and Malibu need a properly prepared and coated finish, and periodic care. We specify the finish based on how exposed your entry actually is rather than applying the same coating everywhere.

How long does a custom iron front door take?

Typically several weeks from approved design to installation, depending on the complexity of the ironwork, the glass, and our current schedule. Because we fabricate in our own Ventura workshop rather than ordering doors in from overseas suppliers, we control the timeline and can adjust a detail mid-build if you change your mind.
