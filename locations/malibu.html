Metalwork Built for Malibu's Salt Air

Ocean air is hard on metal. Salt spray, wind and strong sun can wear down a finish that would last for decades inland, so a gate or railing near the water has to be built and finished differently. For coastal homes we talk through the options before we build: galvanizing under the powder coat, stainless steel or aluminum where it makes sense, stainless hardware, and the finish that fits how close you are to the water. We also tell you honestly what maintenance to expect.

Everything is designed and fabricated in our Ventura shop and installed by our own crew, so one team handles your project from the first measurement to the final adjustment.

What We Build in Malibu

  • Custom Iron Stairs & Railings — Interior and exterior stair railings, deck and balcony railings in steel, stainless, cable or glass
  • Driveway Gates — Swing and sliding gates, manual or motorized, finished for coastal exposure
  • Pedestrian & Side Gates — Entry, courtyard and side-yard gates with stainless hardware
  • Metal Fencing — Perimeter, privacy and pool fencing built to match your gates
  • Custom Iron & Steel Doors — Entry doors built to your opening
  • Repair & Refinishing — Rusted sections replaced, loose railings re-secured and worn finishes redone

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions — Malibu

What metal holds up best near the ocean in Malibu?

It depends on how close you are to the water and how much upkeep you want. Galvanized steel under a powder coat, stainless steel and aluminum all have a place on the coast. We walk through the options for your property, use stainless hardware, and tell you honestly what maintenance each finish needs.

Do I need a permit for a gate or railing in Malibu?

Some projects do and some do not, depending on what you are building and where the property is. Tell us about your project and we will let you know up front what we expect, and we can provide the drawings and specifications your designer or permit process needs.

What does a custom gate or railing cost in Malibu?

Every project is custom, so price depends on size, material, design and finish. We give free on-site estimates with a written quote, and we offer in-house payment plans.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the size and design of the project and whether a permit is involved. We give you a realistic schedule with your quote, before you commit.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Legendary Metal Works Inc. holds California Contractor's License #1119503. You can verify it on the California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) website.

Do you repair existing gates and railings?

Yes. We repair and refinish existing metalwork, including gates and railings we did not build. See our gate and metal repair page for details.
