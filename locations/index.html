Ten Cities, One Shop

Pick Your Area

Each page covers the styles, materials and local conditions that actually come up in that city — slope, salt air, fire zone rules, HOA review.

Ventura, CA

Our home base. The shop is on McGrath Street, so Ventura jobs get the shortest lead times and the easiest follow-up visits.

Oxnard, CA

Coastal work with a marine specification — galvanizing, zinc-rich primer and stainless hardware, because salt air is harder on metal than people expect.

Thousand Oaks, CA

Conejo Valley estates, hillside driveways and HOA architectural review. We have done a lot of all three.

Camarillo, CA

Spanish Hills, Mission Oaks and Camarillo Heights — ornamental iron gates and railings that suit the architecture.

Simi Valley, CA

Wide driveways and sloped approaches, where sliding and cantilever gates usually beat swing.

Moorpark, CA

Ranch fencing, long property lines and driveway gates built for real acreage rather than a suburban lot.

Westlake Village, CA

Gated communities and architectural committees. We build to the standard the neighbourhood expects.

Ojai, CA

Hillside lots, fire zone requirements and a preference for work that looks handmade because it is.

Calabasas, CA

Estate gates, security access control and ornamental ironwork on larger properties.

Malibu, CA

Oceanfront exposure. Everything here gets the coastal package, and it earns its keep.

Not On the List?

We Travel for the Right Job

Those ten cities are where most of our work lands, but they are not a boundary. We regularly install across greater Los Angeles and into Santa Barbara County. If you are outside Ventura County, call and ask — the honest answer depends on the size of the project and how far the crew has to travel, and we will tell you straight rather than quoting you a number that does not work for either of us.
