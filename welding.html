What We Do

Welding & Fabrication Services

Gate & Fence Welding Repair

Broken welds, cracked hinges, sagging frames and rusted-out bottom rails on driveway gates, side gates and iron fences, repaired and refinished.

Railing Repair

Loose posts, broken balusters and failed joints on stair, balcony and deck railings, re-welded and re-anchored so the rail is solid again.

Custom One-Off Pieces

Brackets, frames, plates, supports, planters and signs built to your drawing, sketch or measurements.

Structural Steel

Steel beams, columns and posts for residential projects, fabricated to your engineer's or architect's drawings.

Wrought Iron Restoration

Older iron gates and railings repaired, with missing scrollwork or pickets re-created to match, then refinished.

Fabrication for Contractors

General contractors, remodelers and deck builders: send us the metal part of your job, from railings to custom steel.

Common Questions

Welding & Fabrication FAQs

Do you do welding repairs on gates, railings and fences?

Yes. Broken welds, cracked hinges, loose railing posts and rusted sections are some of the most common jobs we do, including on metalwork we did not build. For installed gates, railings and fences we come to your property.

Can I bring a piece to your shop?

Yes. Our shop is at 4882 McGrath St, Unit 110 in Ventura. Call ahead at (805) 396-9984 so we can look at it with you and give you a price and a time.

Do you build custom one-off pieces?

Yes. Brackets, frames, plates, supports, planters, signs and other custom steel pieces built to your drawing, sketch or measurements. Bring dimensions or a photo and we will work out the details with you.

Do you fabricate structural steel?

We fabricate steel beams, columns and posts for residential projects. For structural work we build to the drawings from your engineer or architect.

How much does welding work cost?

It depends on the piece, the material and how much prep and finishing it needs. Small repairs are often quick. Call or send a photo and we will give you a straight price before we start.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Legendary Metal Works Inc. holds California Contractor's License #1119503. You can verify it on the CSLB website.
